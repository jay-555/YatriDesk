## You may, without asking
- Say truthfully that you use, host or support YatriDesk.
- Refer to the project by name in docs, articles, reviews and comparisons.
- Distribute unmodified official releases under their own name.
- Say that YatriDesk is based on / a fork of the upstream project.

## Modified versions and forks
- Do not present a modified or independently built version as the official
  YatriDesk release, or imply YatriDesk endorses, certifies or supports it.
- Give a fork its own name and its own logo. "Based on YatriDesk" or
  "a fork of YatriDesk" is fine and encouraged.
- Keep the copyright and licence notices (see `NOTICE`) and mark your changes
  and their dates, as the AGPL requires.
- Replace the in-app branding and the source-code link in Settings with your own.

## Ask first
Written permission is needed to use the YatriDesk marks in a product or company
name, domain name, app-store listing, or in a way that suggests an official
relationship. Open an issue at https://github.com/jay-555/YatriDesk/issues.

This policy is a statement of intent for this fork, not legal advice. Trademark
registration status has not been verified.
